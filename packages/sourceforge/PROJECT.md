**Summary (≤70 chars):** Gold calculator: karat prices, making charge, 5% VAT, zakat

**Description:**

Free, offline gold value calculator for the UAE market. Enter today's 24K price per gram (or per ounce) and get 24/22/21/18/14K prices per gram, tola and ounce; the price of a jewellery piece with making charge and 5% VAT; zakat on gold against the 85 g nisab; and conversions between grams, ounces, tolas and mithqals. Arabic (RTL) and English. Available as an Android app, browser extensions, a Docker API, a static web page and libraries + CLIs for JavaScript, Python, Rust, Dart, C#, Ruby and PHP. MIT licensed.

Live UAE gold rates: [سعر الذهب اليوم في الامارات](https://uaegoldprices.ae/)
