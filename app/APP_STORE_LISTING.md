محوّل الوحدات

## English

### Short description (≤ 80)
Gold value by karat, jewellery price with making charge and VAT, and zakat. Offline.

### Full description
The gold calculator from UAE Gold Prices tells you what your gold is worth, in Arabic or English, without an internet connection.

Type today's 24K price once (per gram or per troy ounce) and the app does the rest:

Karat table
24K, 22K, 21K, 18K and 14K prices per gram, tola and ounce, with hallmarks (999, 914, 874, 749, 583).

Jewellery price
Weight, karat and making charge (AED per gram, a percentage of the gold value, or a fixed amount) plus optional 5% VAT, with a clear breakdown of gold value, making, VAT and total.

Zakat on gold
Add pieces of different karats; the app converts them to pure gold, compares the total with the 85 g nisab and shows the 2.5% due.

Unit converter
Gram, kilogram, troy ounce, tola and mithqal, with the value at your price.

Private
No account, no ads, no tracking, and no permissions. Your last price and language stay on your device.

For live gold rates see our site, سعر الذهب اليوم في الامارات: https://uaegoldprices.ae/

Results are estimates. Shop prices, making charges and VAT treatment vary; ask a scholar for zakat rulings.

### Feature bullets
- Every karat per gram, tola and ounce
- Jewellery price with making charge (per gram, percent or fixed)
- Optional 5% VAT
- Zakat across several pieces and karats, 85 g nisab
- Gram, ounce, tola and mithqal converter
- Works offline, asks for no permissions
- Arabic right-to-left and English
- Light and dark Material 3 design
